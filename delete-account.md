# Account Deletion Request

At SubTrackr, we value your privacy and give you full control over your data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you can do so through the methods below.

## Option 1: Delete via the App (Recommended)
The fastest way to delete your account is directly within the SubTrackr application:
1. Open the **SubTrackr** app.
2. Go to the **Settings** tab.
3. Scroll down to the **Danger Zone** section.
4. Tap **Delete Account and Data**.
5. Confirm your choice in the modal that appears.

Your account and all synchronized data will be permanently removed from our servers immediately.

## Option 2: Request via Email
If you no longer have the app installed or prefer to request deletion via email:
1. Send an email to [kelvinetoma95@gmail.com](mailto:kelvinetoma95@gmail.com).
2. Use the subject line: **Account Deletion Request - SubTrackr**.
3. Include the **email address** associated with your SubTrackr account.

Once we receive your request, we will process the deletion and send a confirmation email within 3-5 business days.

## What data will be deleted?
When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:
- Your user profile and authentication records.
- All subscription data synchronized to our cloud database.
- Any personalized settings or configurations.

**Warning:** This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
